Prospective PhD students

Ilia is recruiting PhD students for Fall 2027 at Purdue CS. Apply through the Purdue Computer Science PhD program (applications open Fall 2026); please list Ilia as a potential advisor in your statement. We do not pre-screen applicants by email — the application is the right path.

Prospective postdocs and visiting researchers

Reach out by email with a short note about your research, a CV, and one or two papers you would like to discuss. We do not always have funded positions, but we are happy to support visiting researchers and collaborations.

Undergraduates and master's students

We sometimes have research projects available for students who want to get involved in human–AI research. If you are interested in participating or learning more, please fill out the form below and we'll be in touch.

Media inquiries and invited talks

For media, podcasts, and invited talks, please email the PI directly with a brief description, timing, and venue / outlet.
