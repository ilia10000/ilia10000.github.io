The **AI Thought Partner Lab (AITP Lab)** studies humans and machines in mutually informative ways and builds AI systems that learn and think *with* people rather than *instead of* people. We work at three scales — individually, together, and collectively — across three lines of inquiry:

- **Measuring shared structure** between human and AI minds — across representations, concepts, values, perception, and behavior. Our methods bridge [machine learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13018) and [cognitive science](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-01991-9), and span [efficient learning from very few examples](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/e8ddc03b001d4c4b44b29bc1167e7fdd-Abstract-Conference.html) to large-scale parallel [human–AI experiments](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2308950121).
- **Designing interactive systems** that use shared structure to make human–AI partnership productive — [teaching grounded in alignment](https://openreview.net/forum?id=7zxUVXFPez), legible explanations, calibrated reliance, and uncertainty-aware interfaces.
- **Diagnosing partnership failures** — bias, overreliance, persuasion, miscalibrated trust, and the collective dynamics that emerge when humans and AIs interact at scale, including [Rogers' Paradox in the age of AI](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsta.2025.0149) and [LLM team coordination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12229).

The lab is led by [Ilia Sucholutsky](/people/), currently a Faculty Fellow / Assistant Professor at the NYU Center for Data Science, and starting **August 2026** as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at **Purdue University**.

**Current funding includes** the DARPA "In the Moment" (ITM) program (Algorithmic Trust at Scale, co-PI; 2025–2027), as well as awards from Schmidt Sciences, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, and the Tinker Foundation.
