What we are working on now.

The lab opened at Purdue in August 2026, and the first projects follow the five research stories. On people, we are building systems that learn how one person represents a problem from a handful of interactions, and that show what they are still unsure about, using similarity and triplet judgments as the measurement.

On working with AI, we want to know whether a person is more capable after working with a partner than before. That means pre-registered studies with held-out tasks after the partner is gone, rather than judging the partnership by the quality of its joint output.

On thinking together, we are asking when human-AI partnerships that help each person also help the group discover and correct its errors, and when they fail to compose. The tools are group experiments, agent-based simulations, and measures from the collective-intelligence literature.

Each of these is a place where a new student could start.
